# iMeow — Site CVR Plan

**Goal:** raise conversion rate on imeow.store. Traffic is cold social (Meta), $140 price, skeptical buyers. Two jobs: (1) prove iMeow is a *real, trusted cat brand* (not a dropshipper), (2) remove risk so a cold visitor buys. Profit compounds on consumables (filters/scratchers) → optimize for LTV, not first order.

---

## Priority order (effort → impact)

- **P0 — Funnel integrity.** Nothing else matters if the funnel leaks. (0 ATC on 120 sessions is a break signal.)
- **P1 — Product page buy box.** The sale is won/lost above the fold.
- **P2 — Trust pages** (homepage, founder, contact, policies).
- **P3 — Content + community** (blog/research, UGC gallery).
- **P4 — Upsell/LTV** (subscribe & save, post-purchase, replenishment).

---

## P0 — Funnel integrity (do first, this week)

- **Test ATC → checkout → pay inside the actual TikTok & Instagram in-app browsers** on a real phone. 0 ATC on 120 sessions = likely broken there. This is the single highest-value fix.
- Pull **Meta link clicks vs Shopify sessions** — a gap means clicks aren't landing (CVR bleeding before the page loads).
- **Page speed** on mobile (compress hero video/images, lazy-load). Every second of load kills CVR.
- Turn on **Shop Pay + express checkout** (Apple/Google Pay) — fewer form fields, big mobile CVR lift.

---

## P1 — Product page buy box (above the fold)

- **Price + 2-pack framed:** "$240 for 2 — $120 each, save $40."
- **30-day money-back guarantee + free shipping/returns** (biggest single lever on $140 cold traffic).
- **Star rating + review count** pulled to the top.
- **One-line proof row:** HEPA · under 30dB · covers ___ sq ft · filter lasts ___ months.
- **Sticky ATC button** on mobile.
- **Filter cost, stated plainly** and reframed as proof it works (not a hidden razor-and-blades trap).
- Kill the **"#1 air purifier"** claim (flaggable, reads dropshipper).

---

## P2 — HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE (trust + proof of business, cat-heavy, community)

Each section has one job. Scroll order top → bottom:

1. **Announcement bar** — "Free shipping + 30-day guarantee · Join 10,000+ cat parents" *(only real numbers)*.
2. **Hero** — cat-heavy image/video + emotional promise headline ("The air your cat can't clean themselves"), one-line value prop, star rating, primary CTA. Trust microcopy under button (guarantee · free shipping).
3. **Proof-of-scale strip** — "Trusted by X,000 cat households" · aggregate star rating · any press/badges. Immediate legitimacy.
4. **The problem** — the envy/particle angle from the ads ("what your cat breathes all day"), the visible-dust visual. Ties ad → site so the message matches.
5. **How it works / benefits** — 3–4 tiles with proof markers; show it's a **scratcher + purifier in one**. Each tile = one benefit + one spec.
6. **Founder note (short)** — 3–4 lines + photo with cat, "why we built this," link to full Founder page. Humanizes the brand.
7. **UGC / community wall** — real customer cat photos/videos, "#iMeow" — cats galore + community involvement.
8. **Reviews** — video reviews + written reviews carousel (the ones you already have).
9. **Research/education teaser** — 3 blog cards linking to research pages. Authority + substantiates claims.
10. **Community / giving-back band** — e.g., "We donate to shelters" *(only if true)* — values + trust.
11. **Guarantee / risk-reversal band** — 30-day money-back, free returns, warranty, secure-checkout + payment badges.
12. **FAQ** — top objections (you have these).
13. **Final CTA band** — repeat the offer + CTA.
14. **Footer** — contact, policies (shipping/returns/privacy/terms), about, blog, active social links, trust badges.

> **Honesty rule:** only claim true numbers (customer counts, donations, press). Fake proof is flaggable and kills trust with the exact skeptical buyer you're chasing.

## Blog page + research articles

- **Index** with categories: Cat Health · Air & Home · iMeow Stories.
- **Starter articles** (each substantiates a claim + subtle product tie + CTA), replacing the removed "#1" claim with real evidence:
  1. What your cat actually breathes indoors
  2. Why a cat's nose changes the air-quality math (14× sense of smell)
  3. HEPA and pet dander, explained
  4. The hidden dust of the litter box
  5. Do air purifiers actually help cats?

## UGC / community (the "cat-heavy" engine)

- Homepage wall + dedicated gallery page.
- **Collection:** post-purchase email requesting photos, hashtag #iMeow, small incentive (discount/loyalty points), photo/video reviews app.
- **Community angle:** #iMeowFam, feature customers, shelter partnership.

## P4 — Upsell / LTV (where the profit is)

- **Subscribe & Save** on PDP: filters + scratcher pads auto-ship, ~15% off. Recurring revenue; can raise initial CVR.
- **Post-purchase one-click upsell:** "Add a year of filters" on the thank-you page (highest take-rate placement).
- **Starter-kit tiers:** unit / unit+1yr filters / unit+2yr filters+scratchers.
- **Replenishment flow** (Klaviyo/SMS): "Your filter's due — reorder + 15% off."
