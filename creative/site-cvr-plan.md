# iMeow — Site Plan

Only the pages and sections you asked for: homepage, founder, contact, blog, UGC, and the filter/scratcher upsell.

---

## Homepage (trust + proof of business · cat-heavy · community)

Scroll order, each section one job:

1. **Hero** — cat-heavy image/video + brand promise headline + CTA.
2. **Proof of business / trust** — customer count, aggregate star rating, any press/badges. *(real numbers only)*
3. **Cats / community wall** — UGC: customer cat photos + videos, #iMeow. The cat-heavy, community-driven core.
4. **Founder snippet** — 3–4 lines + photo with cat, links to the full Founder page.
5. **Blog sections** — cards linking to the cat research articles.
6. **Reviews** — your video reviews + testimonials.
7. **FAQ** — the ones you already have.
8. **Footer** — contact, blog, social links.

> Only claim true numbers (customer counts, press). Fake proof is flaggable and repels skeptical buyers.

## Filter & scratcher upsell

- **Subscribe & Save** — filters + scratcher pads auto-ship at a discount.
- **Post-purchase one-click** — "Add a year of filters" on the thank-you page.
- **Starter-kit tiers** — unit / unit + 1yr filters / unit + 2yr filters + scratchers.
- Frame the replacement as proof it works, not a hidden cost.
