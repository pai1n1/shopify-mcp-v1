# GR8 — CBO Ad Build: Muscle angle (1 campaign, 1 ad set, 4 ads)

Structure: CBO, one ad set, four static ads, one angle in four formats, all driving to the sale. Static only until there are winning creatives.

## Campaign, CBO
- Objective: Sales, optimized for purchase. Pixel and Conversions API live first.
- Budget at campaign level, CBO distributes to the winner.
- One ad set, broad and unisex, no gender or detailed targeting, let Andromeda read the creative. Consider excluding Audience Network. Keep a backup ad account, this category gets flagged.

## The angle
You are losing more than fat. On a GLP-1 a real share of what you lose is muscle, and 5g of creatine a day is the most studied way to help hold onto it. Every ad carries this, four ways.

## The four ads (one angle, four formats)

### Ad 1, problem hook
- Job: stop the scroll, name the hidden cost.
- Visual: bag on clean ground, one bold line.
- On image: "ON A GLP-1? YOU'RE LOSING MUSCLE TOO." emphasize MUSCLE.
- Primary text: the drug is working, but a real share of the weight is muscle, and 5g of creatine a day is the most studied way to help keep it. GR8 puts it in your scoop. Soft CTA, see the formula.

### Ad 2, proof and spec
- Job: make it real, justify it.
- Visual: product with thin callout lines to key actives, label forward.
- On image: "5g CREATINE. THE DOSE ACTUALLY STUDIED." emphasize 5g.
- Primary text: a full 5g of creatine monohydrate, not a pinch for the label, plus collagen and the nutrients a GLP-1 strips, one scoop, fully dosed, nothing hidden in a blend. CTA, see what is in it.

### Ad 3, comparison
- Job: kill the "I already take something" objection.
- Visual: two columns, current powder vs GR8, creatine line highlighted.
- On image: "YOUR DAILY POWDER vs GR8."
- Primary text: most daily powders skip the one thing that matters on a GLP-1, real creatine for muscle. Greens fairy dust it or leave it out. GR8 gives a full 5g plus the whole panel, built for people on the drug. CTA, compare them.

### Ad 4, social proof and close
- Job: belief, then the offer.
- Visual: clean review card, verified tag, five stars.
- On image: a short real quote about keeping strength, plus stars.
- Primary text: real people doing GLP-1 the smart way, a true testimonial about keeping muscle or energy, then 5g creatine, full panel, nothing that touches your bloodwork, 30 day money back, cancel anytime. CTA, try GR8. This ad carries the risk reversal since it closes.

## Why the four are cohesive
Same brand look, same angle, same destination, so in any serve order the viewer gets one thread, not noise. Together they walk problem, to proof it is real, to better than what I use, to others trust it, and each one alone still drives to the page and the sale. Each ad adds a new reason, nobody feels spammed. That is the positive experience.

## Compliance guardrails (muscle angle)
- The drug does the weight loss, GR8 protects muscle. Never say lose weight with GR8, never a before and after body.
- Structure and function only, supports muscle, helps you keep, not prevents or treats.
- "On a GLP-1" as context, never "you take Ozempic" as an accusation.
- Testimonials must be real. The Ad 4 quote is a placeholder until there are reviews.

## Reuse
This is the template for every angle. Swap the one angle, keep the four formats, problem hook, proof and spec, comparison, social proof and close.
