# GR8 — Ad Funnel (how the ads are built and work together)

How the campaigns are structured, why, and how each ad relates to the others. This is the architecture. The angle docs supply the creative that fills it.

## The architecture: split by temperature, not by awareness inside one campaign
Two campaigns, built in order over time.
- Campaign 1, cold prospecting (CBO). Reaches strangers. Carries cold creative only.
- Campaign 2, warm retargeting. Reaches people who already visited or engaged. Carries the offer and proof.

The split is by how warm the person already is, because that is the only reliable control we have.

## Why not put every awareness level in one campaign
You do not choose which ad a person sees. Meta does. And Meta does not walk a person up an awareness journey. For every single impression it makes one greedy bet: of these ads, which is most likely to get a purchase from this person right now. So:
- One mixed campaign is not a set sequence and not a smart sequence. It is a per-impression bet, repeated.
- It will show the most-aware offer ad to a stone-cold stranger if it guesses that converts, with no ad guaranteed to have warmed them first.
- A set sequence guarantees order. One mixed campaign guarantees no order.

So the reliable sequence is built with audiences, not hoped for inside one campaign. The cold campaign warms them, the retargeting campaign closes them, and the offer only reaches people the cold campaign already touched.

## Repetition versus awareness (two different gains)
- Frequency, seeing the ad again, builds familiarity, recognition and trust. It moves them from never heard of you to I have seen these people around.
- Message, the actual copy, builds understanding and belief, which climbs the awareness ladder.
You need both. Ten exposures of the wrong message does not make anyone product-aware. The higher the price and the deeper the skepticism, the more touches it takes, and GR8 is a considered health buy, so it is many touches, not a few.

## How each ad works with the others
- One ad, one job. If an ad cannot state its job in a line, it is not ready.
- Every ad stands alone and drives to the sale. Because Meta still picks the order inside a campaign, no ad can depend on another being seen first.
- Cohesion. Same brand look, same angle, same destination, so in any serve order the viewer gets one thread, not noise. Each ad adds a new reason, nobody feels spammed. That is the positive experience.
- Diversity means different, not more. Different angles and formats between ads, never a bigger pile of the same ad.
- Awareness maps to temperature. Cold gets problem-aware, solution-aware, and the fork. Warm gets product-aware and most-aware, the offer and the social proof.

Self-contained ads are the fine control, the temperature split is the coarse one. Use both.

## The two filters
- Filter A, before launch, you judge taste, free. Generate rough ads, keep what looks right and reads right, lock the look and the copy voice, then stop.
- Filter B, after launch, the market judges performance, paid. Launch, let data pick winners, kill losers, scale proof.
Never launch raw ideas. Never overthink past a locked look.

## Campaign 1, the cold CBO (the first launch)
| Setting | What it is |
|---|---|
| Objective | Sales, optimized for purchase. Pixel and CAPI live first |
| Budget | Campaign level, CBO distributes it |
| Audience | Broad and unisex, no detailed targeting, let Andromeda read the creative. Exclude Audience Network |
| Ad set | One to start, one before two |
| Creative | Cold ads only, problem-aware, solution-aware, and the fork. No offer or proof ad |

What it tests: which of the three angles converts a stranger. Muscle, Looks, or Energy. CBO sorts them and tells you which problem your buyer feels hardest. That is the concept proof.

Lean build, not the full set: three cold ads per angle, the problem, the solution, and the fork, nine ads in one broad ad set. Find the winning angle, then build its full set.

## Campaign 2, retargeting (added once there is a warm pool)
Turned on after traffic and the pixel build an audience. Reaches site visitors, add to cart, engagers. Carries product-aware and most-aware creative, the fork, the proof, and the offer. These only make sense to someone who already knows GR8.

## Phasing and the rule
Day one you have no warm pool and no pixel data, so there is nobody to retarget. Launch the cold campaign only. Once traffic and the pixel build an audience, turn on retargeting. Scale on proof, add a second ad set or campaign only after the first converts and pays for it.
