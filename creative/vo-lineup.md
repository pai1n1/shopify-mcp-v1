# iMeow — VO Lineup (to use)

**Angle:** envy → provider guilt → absolution. Same visual world as `bolt` (winner).
**Pun:** "the little things… *literally*" — hard cut / push-in to floating dust + dander on the word "literally." Say "literally" flat and deadpan, after a full beat. The particles are the punchline.
**Read:** calm, matter-of-fact female, ~2.2 words/sec. Do NOT match the sadness — contrast reads as credible.
**End card must carry the CTA** (price + free shipping/returns + one proof marker — HEPA · under 30dB · coverage sq ft — + "Order now → imeow.store"). The VO closes on emotion, not instruction.

## A1 — "Every fur parent" (gift close)
> Every fur parent does their best — and sometimes your best still misses the little things… *literally.* The dust, the dander, the air she breathes all day. Now you can give her the one thing you never could: clean air.

## B — "You'd do anything" (enough close)
> You'd do anything for her — and sometimes it's the little things you can't see that she needs most… *literally.* Dust, dander, litter, the fur she grooms off her own coat. Now you've done everything you can — and that's enough.

## C — "You love her" (love-reaches close)
> You love her more than anything — and sometimes love can't catch the little things floating in her air… *literally.* The stuff she breathes every day, in the room she can't leave. Now your love reaches the one place it couldn't: her air.

### Notes
- Endings are swappable across all three (gift / enough / love-reaches) — keep the chosen end consistent within a test set so you're only isolating the hook.
- A1 = clearest product close. B = strongest "best fur parent / you did enough" reassurance. C = softest, most emotional.
- Deploy A1/B as **VO+CC** (highest expected). Consistent end line across the set.
