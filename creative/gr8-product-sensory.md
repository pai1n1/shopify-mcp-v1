# GR8 — Product Sensory Priorities (locked)

The three focus points, in order, before formula density:

1. Solubility
2. Flavor
3. Color

## Why these come first
This is a daily drink on subscription. The experience of drinking it, how it mixes, tastes and looks, is what keeps a subscriber past the first renewal, and the renewal is where the $69 finally pays back. Competitors' loudest cancellations were taste and grit, so the sensory experience is a retention lever, not a cosmetic one.

## Rules
- Sample and taste before any production run. Never pay a deposit on a flavor, mix or color that has not been sampled and approved in person. A spec sheet cannot tell you if it is good.
- The formula flexes for drinkability. Spirulina and chlorella and the heavy mineral load are the enemies of all three, taste, solubility and color. If they wreck any of them, cut or lower them. A simpler formula people love and keep drinking beats a denser one they quit.
- Taste is engineered, not lucky. Ask the factory for two or three sample iterations and tell them taste is the number one requirement.

## Solubility
- Must dissolve clean in water, no grit, no sludge at the bottom. This was a top complaint on competitors and it makes everything taste worse.

## Flavor
- Mask the base with flavorless agents, then layer the real flavor on top so it tastes like the flavor, not like a cover up: a bitter blocker, a pinch of salt, a touch of natural acid, light monk fruit.
- Natural flavors only. No artificial flavors or dyes. No sugar alcohols, they cause GI issues.
- Keep it light and bright, not dessert sweet, because the buyer often has nausea and dulled taste.
- Flavors: Lemonade as the year round hero. One rotating seasonal exotic as the second, for example watermelon in summer, blood orange in the cold months. Go bright, tart or tropical, never creamy over this base. Launch with Lemonade alone to keep the buy in low, add the seasonal once sales are coming in.

## Color
- Must look appetizing and clean, not swamp green from the algae. Aim for a light, natural color that matches the flavor. Color is another reason to keep the spirulina and chlorella low.
