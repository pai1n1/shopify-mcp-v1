# iMeow — Homepage Design Brief (for the Claude design session)

**Your job:** design the **homepage** for imeow.store — visual structure, layout, hierarchy, and sample copy. Output a designed page (sections + UI), mobile-first.

**Scope:** HOMEPAGE ONLY. Do **not** design the product page (PDP) — that's handled separately. Another session implements your design in Shopify afterward.

> **You can ask me questions.** For facts you don't have (real review counts, specs, founder story), use clearly-marked `[placeholders]` — never invent numbers.

---

## The point of this page

**Build trust and raise conversion.** Traffic is cold Meta social, $139 product, skeptical buyers. The homepage's job is to make a first-time visitor believe iMeow is a **real, trusted, cat-loving brand** worth $139 — fast.

**Product:** a cat air purifier that doubles as a scratcher. It quietly pulls dust, dander, and litter particles out of the air a cat breathes all day.

---

## Design direction

- **Colorful, vibrant, cat-lover, community-forward — but PREMIUM in execution.** Color does not cheapen a page; *cheap execution* does (clip-art, clashing colors, low-res images, clutter, inconsistent type). Hold the line on craft: **one intentional palette, generous whitespace, high-quality photography, consistent type system.**
- **Scent-match the ads.** Our ads use a warm, playful, Pixar-style animated cat. Carry that world in: **use the animated cat as a mascot/illustration accent** and echo the ad's warm palette, so someone clicking the ad instantly feels they're in the right place.
- **Imagery:** real lifestyle content — **cats AND people, photo AND video** (real homes, real cat parents). Product shown in **premium real photography**. Mascot used as accent, not the whole page.
- **Voice:** warm, matter-of-fact; emotional angle is envy → provider guilt → absolution ("give them the one thing you couldn't"). Refer to the cat as **they/them** (unisex).

---

## Homepage structure

The page runs an alternating **claim → proof → claim → proof** rhythm — every lifestyle claim is immediately backed by proof. Design it top to bottom:

| # | Section | Job | Copy direction (sample) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero — cat w/ product** | Emotional promise + scent-match + CTA | "The one thing your cat can't clean themselves. **Their air.**" · sub-line · **[Shop iMeow →]** · microcopy: free shipping · [30-day guarantee] |
| 2 | **Social proof** | Fast credibility | "[4.8★] from [1,200+] reviews · [10,000+] cat households" |
| 3 | **Cat w/ product — how it works** | The mechanism | "Clean air, on autopilot." HEPA pulls dust, dander, litter; scratcher + purifier in one; proof row: HEPA · under 30dB · covers [__] sq ft · filter lasts [__] mo |
| 4 | **Social proof — UGC wall** | Community | "The iMeow family" — grid of real customer cat + owner photos/videos, #iMeow |
| 5 | **Product** | The buy moment | Premium product photography, what's in the box, price tiers, [guarantee], Add to Cart |
| 6 | **Social proof — video reviews** | Believability | "Hear it from real cat parents" — video review carousel |
| 7 | **Cat w/ product — lifestyle / founder** | Brand + community | Real cat + owner lifestyle; short founder note + photo; "Join the family" + CTA |
| 8 | **Social proof — reviews** | Close | Written review carousel + rating + final CTA → FAQ → footer (contact · blog · our story · socials) |

**Rules:**
- **Each "cat w/ product" block = ONE distinct message** (hero promise / how it works / lifestyle) — don't repeat the same pitch.
- **Vary the proof TYPE** across the four proof slots (rating strip → UGC wall → video reviews → written reviews). Four identical review carousels reads as padding.
- **Scent match lives in block 1** — mascot + ad palette + the "clean air" promise.
- **CTA cadence + sticky mobile CTA** — the buy button should be reachable throughout the scroll, not only in section 5.
- **Mobile-first.** Most traffic is mobile in-app; design for the phone first.
