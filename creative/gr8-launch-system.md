# GR8 — Launch Execution System

The map out of the planning loop. Three phases, two filters, hard gates. Follow it in order, ship.

## Phase 0, Foundations (all in parallel, the only thing blocking launch)
| Item | Done when |
|---|---|
| Website with the locked branding | live, loads, checkout works |
| Ad account plus one backup | both active, business verified |
| Pixel and CAPI | firing, a test purchase tracked |
| Supplement-friendly payment processor | approved, can take a charge |
| Social profiles, IG, TikTok, FB | created, branded, link in bio |
| Legal pages and supplement facts | published |

Rule: none of this has to be perfect, it has to be live. The website and the creative run at the same time, do not wait on each other.

## Phase 1, Creative R&D (Filter A, taste not spend)
Goal: find the visual and the words that land, cheaply, before building the real set. Generate many rough ads across the three angles. Judge only two things, does the visual look right and do the words hit. Keep what lands, kill the rest.
Done when: the visual style is locked and the copy voice is locked per angle. Stop generating the second you have them. This is where the doom loop hides, so this gate is hard.

## Phase 2, Build the campaign set (the usable ads)
Goal: turn the locked look and words into the funnel. Three angles. 15 to 20 cohesive ads per angle, laddered across awareness, one job each, every ad tagged Job, Angle, Awareness, Avatar, all driving the one sale.
Done when: the full set is built, tagged, and loaded.

## Phase 3, Launch and prove (Filter B, the market)
Goal: prove concept and brand with real money. CBO, one campaign, broad and unisex, purchase objective. Launch, let data pick winners, kill losers, scale the proof.
Done when: one profitable or clearly promising angle. That is proof.

## The two filters
- Filter A, before launch, judge taste, free.
- Filter B, after launch, the market judges performance, paid.
Never launch raw ideas. Never overthink past a locked look.

## The three launch angles (approved)
1. Muscle. Keep your strength, not just lose the scale. Already built in gr8-ad-cbo-muscle.md.
2. Looks. Keep your face and hair, not gaunt and deflated.
3. Energy and nutrients. Feel human again when you are barely eating.

Each angle runs the same four-format, awareness-laddered frame. Swap the angle, keep the structure.
